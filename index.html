QUANTUM LEVEL

Electron orbitals

s-Orbital

A sphere with electrons equally likely to be found in every direction. Each energy level has one s-orbital (maximum 2 electrons).

p-Orbital

Dumbbell-shaped, with 3 orientations (px, py, pz). Each p-orbital holds 2 electrons, for a total of 6 in the p-subshell.

Electron filling

Electrons fill orbitals according to the Aufbau principle, starting at the lowest energy level and moving upward. Each orbital can hold up to 2 electrons with opposite spins.

CHEMISTRY LEARNING

About the game and chemistry concepts

Molecule Master is an interactive chemistry learning game. Build molecules from atoms and explore bonds, ions, reactions, and material properties. The game is a learning aid, not a laboratory procedure or a substitute for authoritative chemical data.

Molecule
A molecule is an electrically neutral particle made up of two or more atoms joined by chemical bonds.
Covalent bond
A covalent bond is a chemical bond in which atoms share one or more pairs of electrons.
Ion
An ion is an atom or group of atoms with an electric charge, formed by gaining or losing electrons.
Molar mass
Molar mass is the mass of one mole of a substance; a common unit is grams per mole (g/mol).
Chemical reaction
In a chemical reaction, bonds between reactant atoms change and products form; the number of atoms of each element is conserved.

Sources and contact

For authoritative chemistry definitions and substance data, consult these specialist sources:

  • IUPAC Gold Book – chemistry terminology and definitions.
  • PubChem – a database of substances, molecules, and chemical properties.
  • NIST Chemistry WebBook – thermochemical and spectral data for selected substances.
